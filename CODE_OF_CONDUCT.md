# Code of Conduct

Lime is a small project, and it should be a friendly place to ask questions, report bugs and send changes.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Mac development or to open source.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no to a feature that doesn't fit the app.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a home address, email or real name, without their permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a request after it was declined.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, with a title starting with `Conduct:`. Only the maintainer can read it. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
